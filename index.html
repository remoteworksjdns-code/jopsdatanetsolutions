What we do

Our services

End-to-end on-site support for data centre and interconnect equipment, delivered by engineers who know the floor.

Rack & Stack / Installations

Physical installation of servers, network and optical equipment, with neat cabling, labelling and documentation to your standards.

Smart Hands Support

On-site remote hands for troubleshooting, reseats, power cycles, cable patching, card and optic swaps, and visual checks.

Storage & Delivery

Secure storage of your equipment and timely delivery to data centre sites when and where you need it.

Spares Management

Tracking, stocking and handling of critical spares so replacement parts are ready to minimise downtime.

Service Provisioning & Testing

Cross-connect and circuit provisioning with thorough testing and verification before handover.

Monitoring & Reporting

Ongoing monitoring of equipment health with clear, regular reports so you always know the status of your assets.
